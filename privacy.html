This policy explains how the Exam Tracker mobile app (“the app”) handles your information.

Information the app accesses

  • Google account basics: your name, email address and profile picture, provided when you sign in with Google. They are used to show who is signed in and to keep each account's data separate on your device.
  • Exam details you enter: exam name, category, date, center name and address, notes, and any document you attach.

Where your data is stored

  • On your phone, so the app works offline.
  • In a Google Sheet named “Exam Tracker” and, for attachments, files in your own Google Drive. The app uses the Google Drive “drive.file” permission, which lets it access only files it created itself. It cannot see your other Drive files.

We do not operate any server or database and we do not receive, store or have access to your exam data.

How the data is used

Only to provide the app's features: showing your exams and syncing them to your Google Sheet and Drive. We do not sell, rent or share your data, we do not use it for advertising, and the app contains no analytics or ad tracking. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deletion

  • Sign out from the app menu at any time.
  • Delete an exam in the app to remove it from the Google Sheet (its attached file is moved to your Drive trash).
  • You can delete the “Exam Tracker” sheet and files from Google Drive yourself at any time.
  • Revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
