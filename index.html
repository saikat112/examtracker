What is Exam Tracker?

Exam Tracker is a mobile app that helps you keep your exam dates, exam centers and admit cards organised. It supports categories such as GATE, State PSC / UPSC, Banking / IBPS and Railway RRB.

Features

  • Add, edit and delete exams with date, center name and address
  • Attach your admit card or any PDF / image (up to 5 MB)
  • Search and filter by category and sync status
  • See your next upcoming exam and the days remaining
  • Works offline and syncs when you are online

Your data stays in your Google account

You sign in with Google. The app creates a Google Sheet called “Exam Tracker” in your own Google Drive and stores your exams there. We do not run a server and cannot see your data. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
